The 6-Step ABG Interpretation Guide

Master clinical assessment with this systematic sequential framework.

Masterclass
1

Assess the pH

Check if the pH is normal (7.35 - 7.45). Below 7.35 indicates acidosis, while above 7.45 indicates alkalosis.

2

Evaluate PaCO2 (Respiratory)

Examine carbon dioxide levels (35 - 45 mmHg). High PaCO2 signals respiratory acidosis; low PaCO2 signals respiratory alkalosis.

3

Evaluate HCO3- (Metabolic)

Examine bicarbonate levels (22 - 26 mEq/L). Low HCO3- indicates metabolic acidosis; high HCO3- indicates metabolic alkalosis.

4

Match Primary Disorder (ROME)

Use ROME: Respiratory Opposite (pH and PaCO2 move opposite) / Metabolic Equal (pH and HCO3- move same).

5

Determine Compensation

Check if opposing system is trying to correct pH: Uncompensated, Partially Compensated, or Fully Compensated.

6

Assess Oxygenation (PaO2)

Review PaO2 (80 - 100 mmHg) to grade hypoxemia status (normal, mild, moderate, or severe).

Understanding Compensation States

Learn how the body responds to maintain homeostasis during acid-base imbalances.

Acute / Recent

1. Uncompensated

The primary system has created an imbalance, but the opposing system has not responded yet. Its value remains entirely normal.

Lab Picture:

• pH: Abnormal (below 7.35 or above 7.45)

• Primary Component: Abnormal

• Secondary Component: Normal

Active Response

2. Partially Compensated

The opposing system has recognized the imbalance and started working to fix it, but it hasn't pushed the pH all the way back into the normal range yet.

Lab Picture:

• pH: Still Abnormal

• Primary Component: Abnormal

• Secondary Component: Abnormal

Corrected pH

3. Fully Compensated

The opposing system has worked overtime and successfully dragged the pH back into the normal range (7.35 - 7.45), even though the underlying problem is still present.

Lab Picture:

• pH: Returned to Normal (7.35 - 7.45)

• Both PaCO2 & HCO3-: Abnormal

• Tip: Check pH lean (below 7.40 vs above 7.40) to find primary disorder!

Quick Memory Aid: ROME

Mastery Cheat Sheet

⚡ RESPIRATORY = OPPOSITE (pH and PaCO2 move in opposite directions)

⚡ METABOLIC = EQUAL (pH and HCO3- move in the same direction)
